What’s included and excluded

Power BI package covers 3–5 report pages, agreed sources and KPIs, modelling/DAX, validation, handover and two revision rounds. Monthly support (optional) covers one agreed refresh and up to two hours of minor changes.

Website package covers up to five public pages, responsive design, basic enquiry form, client-supplied content, basic technical SEO, deployment help and two revision rounds. Authentication, complex third-party integrations and custom applications are quoted separately. Payment integration work is available under the Payment Integration & Business Automation service.

Website care (optional, £39–£59/month) covers agreed maintenance and dependency updates, basic uptime checks, up to 30 minutes of minor content changes monthly, and a response target of three business days.

Monthly Power BI reporting (optional, £150–£300/month) covers one agreed refresh and up to two hours of minor changes.

Excluded unless agreed in writing: third-party subscriptions, hosting, domains, Power BI licences, stock images, and complex third-party integrations.

Engagement process

  1. Enquiry via email with a short description of what you need.
  2. Scope agreement: we confirm deliverables, timeline and price in writing.
  3. Deposit (typically 30–50% for fixed-scope work) to start.
  4. Development and regular check-ins.
  5. Review and consolidated revision rounds.
  6. Final payment and handover of files, access and documentation.

Ongoing plans require one month’s notice to cancel. Deposits, cancellations and refunds are governed by the agreed project terms and applicable UK consumer law. Any work completed before cancellation will be handled in accordance with those terms and legal rights. These are practical commercial terms, not a formal legal contract.
