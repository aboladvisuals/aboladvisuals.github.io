GrandStay Hospitality — Operations Intelligence (Power BI case study)

Data & BI · Excel · Power Query · Power BI

A portfolio case study that builds a measurable time, scale and cost baseline for 120,000 guest-support tickets at a fictional global hotel group, before any decision to buy an automated “travel concierge”. Built in Excel, Power Query, PivotTables and Power BI.

GitHub repositoryFull findings write-up
TypePortfolio case study (not a client engagement)
ToolsExcel · Power Query · PivotTables · Power BI · PowerPoint
Grain1 row per ticket (120,000) + 12 monthly cost rows
PeriodTickets 9 Jul–7 Oct 2025; costs Nov 2024–Oct 2025

Problem

Guest support was under pressure: slow first response, missed SLAs, repetitive guest questions, rising cost per contact, falling utilisation and rising overtime. Leadership was considering an Intelligent Travel Concierge. This project measured the baseline first, so that any automation decision could be judged against facts rather than a vendor pitch.

Findings

  • Time. 72% of tickets missed SLA. Phone and Chat fail because their targets are 1.5–2 minutes, not because they are slower than Email.
  • Scale. 60% of tickets are flagged repetitive and take 10.8 minutes of handle time, versus 29.1 for other work.
  • Cost. Volume rose 39% while cost per contact rose and utilisation fell.
  • Automation. “Minutes saved” in the knowledge sample is largest on Legal, Billing, Emergency, Complaint and Contract tickets only because those tickets are already long, so it should not be used to rank automation candidates.

Recommendations

  1. Staff and route Chat and Phone in peak hours before buying a new tool; keep Email on its 30-minute target.
  2. Pilot a concierge on repetitive FAQ categories on Chat, with a human handoff for non-standard requests.
  3. Do not automate Legal, Emergency, Complaint, Contract or Billing first.
  4. Review roster against peak demand rather than hiring 1:1 with monthly ticket growth.
  5. Report median FRT and FRT by channel instead of a single 10-minute company average.

Limits

No CSAT conclusion is drawn. The knowledge file is a sample. Cost cannot be sliced by channel. No bot was implemented, and no hours-saved or money-saved outcome exists in this dataset. The .pbix file and the 50 MB Excel workbook are intentionally kept off GitHub.
